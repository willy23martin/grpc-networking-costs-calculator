How SAGA affects your gRPC traffic

RPS vs. SAGA steps are different concepts. Your RPS (e.g. 1,000 req/s) measures how many logical business requests arrive per second — for example, 1,000 customers placing an order per second.

Each of those orders triggers one SAGA instance, which internally coordinates multiple microservices. Every step in that SAGA instance is a separate gRPC call to a different service:

↳ Compensatable step (e.g. Reserve inventory) — can be undone if a later step fails.
Pivot step (e.g. Charge payment) — point of no return.
Retriable step (e.g. Ship order) — guaranteed to eventually succeed.

So the formula is multiplicative, not additive:

effective gRPC traffic = base RPS × steps per SAGA instance

Example: 1,000 RPS × 3 steps = 3,000 gRPC calls/sec — not 1,000 + 3,000 = 4,000. The 3 steps replace the single call; each of the 1,000 requests fans out into 3 independent calls.
